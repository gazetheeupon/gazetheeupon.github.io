Privacy Policy

RunLocal (published at gazetheeupon.github.io) is a set of free browser-based utilities. This page explains what the site itself collects and how third-party analytics and advertising work here.

Last updated: September 17, 2026

The short version

The files you process with any RunLocal tool never leave your browser. Every converter, generator, and viewer on this site runs entirely client-side in JavaScript. There is no server that receives your files, your passwords, or the data you type into a tool — there is no upload step at all. That is true regardless of the choices described below.

What this policy covers instead is standard website analytics and advertising: how many people visit, which pages they came from, and the ads shown alongside the tools that keep this site free.

Analytics: Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate traffic: which pages are visited, how often, and from roughly where. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives standard technical data (such as your browser type, approximate location derived from IP address, and the pages you view on this site). It does not receive anything you type into or upload to a tool.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all sites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by choosing "Reject non-essential" in this site's cookie banner, which prevents the Analytics script from loading at all.

Advertising: Google AdSense & A-Ads

This site is supported by ads served through Google AdSense and A-Ads (a-ads.com). These are third-party advertising networks, and each may set its own cookies or use similar technologies to serve ads and measure their performance. Depending on your consent choice and settings, ads may be personalized based on your activity across sites, or shown without personalization.

To control ad personalization:

  • Use the cookie banner on this site to reject non-essential cookies, which prevents AdSense and A-Ads scripts from loading in the first place.
  • Visit Google Ads Settings to control how Google personalizes ads for you across the web.
  • Visit aboutads.info (US) or youronlinechoices.eu (EU) for broader opt-outs across participating ad networks.

A-Ads publishes its own policy at a-ads.com/privacy-policy.

Your consent choice

On your first visit, a banner asks you to Accept or Reject non-essential. This choice is stored locally in your browser (not on any server) and controls whether Google Analytics, Google AdSense, and the A-Ads ad are loaded on subsequent pages:

  • Accept loads Analytics and ads, and allows ad personalization signals.
  • Reject non-essential loads none of them; no analytics or advertising scripts run for the rest of your visit.

You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, which will show the banner again on your next visit.

What this site does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no passwords collected.
  • No file uploads to any server for any tool on this site.
  • No sale of personal information.
  • No tracking of file contents, filenames, or data you enter into a tool.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as tools or third-party services change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
